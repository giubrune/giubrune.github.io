The DPU module couples HYDRUS's description of water flow and solute transport in the soil with a dynamic model of the plant. With this coupling, a single physically based framework can simulate how chemicals are taken up by roots, translocated through the plant, and transformed.

The module was introduced in *Water Resources Research* (2019). Later work used it to study pharmaceuticals such as carbamazepine in green pea plants (ES&T, 2021) and the fate of ionizable compounds in the soil–plant continuum (J. Hazardous Materials, 2022).
