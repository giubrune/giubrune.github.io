The Furrow module couples water flow and solute transport along an irrigation furrow with variably saturated flow and transport in the soil simulated by HYDRUS. It can be used to analyse furrow irrigation and fertigation scenarios.

The numerical approach, a hybrid finite volume–finite element scheme, is described in *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture* (2018).
