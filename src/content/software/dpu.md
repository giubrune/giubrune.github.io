Standard soil models stop at the root surface. The Dynamic Plant Uptake module couples HYDRUS with a dynamic model of the plant, so that chemicals taken up with soil water can be followed into roots, stems, leaves and fruits, where they may also be transformed. It is used to study, for example, the uptake of pharmaceuticals by crops.
