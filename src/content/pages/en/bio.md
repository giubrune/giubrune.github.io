Giuseppe Brunetti is Associate Professor at the Department of Civil Engineering of the University of Calabria in Rende, Italy. His research fields are soil physics and vadose zone hydrology, numerical modelling of transport processes in unsaturated porous media, and Bayesian inference, model complexity analysis and uncertainty assessment.

He received his Ph.D. from the University of Calabria in 2017. He was a visiting scholar at the University of California, Riverside, and the Technical University of Dresden, and a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Brescia, the University of California, Davis, and the Czech University of Life Sciences in Prague. From 2018 to 2023 he worked at the University of Natural Resources and Life Sciences (BOKU) in Vienna, first as Assistant Professor and then as Senior Research Scientist and Lecturer. He joined the University of Calabria as tenure-track Associate Professor in 2023.

He is a co-developer of several HYDRUS modules and of the pHydrus Python library. He won the “Rita Levi Montalcini” Young Researchers Program in 2022 and the International Soil Modeling Consortium Early Career Award in 2018, and is an associate editor of the *Journal of Hydrology* and *Vadose Zone Journal*, among other journals.
