This is a personal academic website hosted on GitHub Pages.

- The site sets **no cookies**, runs **no analytics or tracking**, and embeds no third-party content. Fonts are served from the site itself.
- Your light/dark theme choice is saved only in your own browser (local storage) and is never sent anywhere.
- GitHub, the hosting provider, may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
