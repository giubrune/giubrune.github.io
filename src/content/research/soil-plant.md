Plants take up water from the soil, and with it substances dissolved in that water. To know where these substances end up, the soil and the plant have to be described together.

## In more detail

This theme covers mechanistic plant uptake, the translocation of pharmaceuticals and ionizable compounds, and water and carbohydrate allocation in fruits.

Giuseppe co-developed the Dynamic Plant Uptake (DPU) module for HYDRUS. His keynote at the 7th International Conference “HYDRUS Software Applications to Subsurface Flow and Contaminant Transport Problems” (Prague, 2023) was titled “Re-greening HYDRUS: Towards a unified physically-based soil-plant model”. As a postdoctoral researcher at the Czech University of Life Sciences in Prague (2018), he worked on modeling processes in the soil–water–plant environment.
